# Tile key (4-char tile notation)
  =  water (≈)      .  grass       #  rock (🪨)     P  pony (🐴)
  @  portal (🌀)    B  bee (🐝)    A  apple (🍎)    C  cherry (🍒)
  : prefix - enclosed tile (yellow background) — appears once pen closes
  □/▣ - empty/filled rock slot (in header)
